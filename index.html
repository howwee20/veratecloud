FAQ

The straightforward version.

What is PolySwap?+

A harness that lets different intelligences work inside one coherent context.

Does it replace my AI subscriptions?+

No. PolySwap is designed to bring the access you already have—subscriptions, APIs, providers, and local models—into one place.

Is PolySwap a coding app?+

No. Coding is one kind of work. PolySwap is for research, creation, browsing, files, and whatever intelligence work becomes next.

Does every model work today?+

Not yet. PolySwap is early, and availability depends on the provider and connection method. The harness is designed to grow as intelligence does.

What happens when I switch models?+

The next intelligence receives the job’s context and current state so the work can continue without starting over.

Where can I use PolySwap?+

Download PolySwap for macOS or Windows, or open the phone experience.
