FAQ

The straightforward version.

What is PolySwap?+

A general-purpose agentic computer for working with different AI models in one continuous job.

Is PolySwap a coding app?+

No. PolySwap is designed for general work across conversations, files, browsers, and tools.

Which intelligence can I use?+

PolySwap is built to work with models from providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, and Moonshot AI.

What happens when I switch models?+

The next intelligence receives the job’s explicit context and current state so the work can continue.

Where can I use PolySwap?+

Download PolySwap for macOS or Windows, or open the phone experience.
